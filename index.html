Why Contexto

Generic AI starts from zero. Contexto starts from your work.

Generic AI

A blank page every time

It does not know who you are, who your clients are, or what happened last week. You spend more time explaining than working, and the output is generic.

Contexto

Connected to your work from day one

It knows your clients, your documents and your history. Ask anything about your business and get an answer like you would from a senior colleague who was there for all of it.

Shared memory

One memory for the team. Across every project.

What one colleague teaches Contexto, everyone's Contexto knows. Your firm's background and the status of each project live in one shared place, so nobody starts from a blank page or repeats the same briefing.

01

Firm notes

Who you are, how you work, your clients and your standards. Written once, available to every user in your organization.

02

Project status

Link a project folder once and Contexto keeps a shared status for it. Anyone's update is visible to everyone, and two people editing at the same time never overwrite each other.

03

You approve what it remembers

Contexto reads recent sessions and suggests what is worth remembering. Nothing is saved until you share, edit or dismiss each suggestion, and you can delete any entry later.

Private to your firmShared notes are encrypted with a key that belongs to your organization alone. Other customers can never read them, and they are treated as information, never as instructions.
What it does

Built-in skills, ready from day one.

01

Reads your documents

Ask questions about any file directly. Contexto reads Word, Excel, PDF and PowerPoint, including scanned pages, in Hebrew and English, and works through long files page by page.

  • Word
  • Excel
  • PDF and scans
  • PowerPoint
02

Drafts in your style

Writes Word documents on your own templates and keeps their fonts and styles, with correct right-to-left layout for Hebrew. It previews the result in Word to check it before you see it. Emails are drafts for you to review. Contexto never sends on its own.

  • Your templates
  • Hebrew right to left
  • Word preview
  • You approve every send
03

Meeting notes

Turns rough notes or transcripts into clear action items and decisions, ready to share.

  • Action items
  • Decisions
  • Follow-ups
04

Keeps watch, keeps the thread

Watches the folders you choose and tells you when something new arrives. Reopen any session from the last week and carry on exactly where you stopped.

  • Watched folders
  • Tray alerts
  • Resume sessions
05

Honest about what it sees

When a quote comes from a scan, Contexto says it is a reading, not a verified copy. Ask whether a document is genuine and it checks for signs of editing, signatures and QR codes, then tells you clearly what it could not prove.

  • Scan readings
  • Authenticity checks
  • Clear limits
06

Everyday extras

Researches current information online, sees what you see in the browser, creates invoices from your template, and reads your mail, Gmail and WhatsApp Web to help you reply, with sending always left to you.

  • Web research
  • Private browser
  • Invoices
  • Mail, Gmail, WhatsApp Web
Security

Private by design. Managed for you.

Your clients trust you with their information. Contexto is built so that trust does not have to be traded away to use AI.

Your own account

Every firm gets its own private account, separate from every other customer.

Files stay with you

Your files stay on your computer. Only what a request needs is sent, encrypted, to the AI.

Never used for training

Your data is never used to train models and never shared with third parties.

You stay in control

Contexto asks before it acts on your computer, and drafts never leave until you send them.
